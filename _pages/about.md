Research
------
My research interests revolve around how to best analyse, predict, and control chaotic dynamical systems when only imperfect models and noisy data are available. This extends from study into data-driven dynamical systems to data assimilation. Figures and animations arising from my research can be viewed in [Research](https://dankesean.github.io/research/). Information about research papers and presentations can be found in [Publications](https://dankesean.github.io/publications/) and [Talks](https://dankesean.github.io/talks/).


Teaching
------
Throughout my study I have had a number of professional roles including tutoring, course coordinating, and course developing. These include roles across Mathematics and Engineering faculties at the University of Adelaide. More information can be found in [Teaching](https://dankesean.github.io/teaching/).
